# <The problem, in eight words or fewer>

- **Severity:** <High, Medium, or Low>. **Effort:** <Small, Medium, or Large.>
- **Kind:** <Fix after merge, Needs decision, Outside the scope, or Post-merge task.>
- **Added:** <YYYY-MM-DD>, from <the branch or the pull request, and the review finding number when there is one.>
- **Location:** <The file path and line.>
- **Reason:** <One sentence: why the work waits until after the merge.>

<The body depends on the kind:

- A review finding: the finding in the finding layout of the
  `decisions-and-findings` skill, from **Terms** to **Action**.
- A blocked wider fix: the wider fix, its options, the finding numbers that
  it covers, and the previous examples. Its kind is Needs decision.
- A problem outside the review scope: its line from the review report.
- A post-merge task: the task, and what it needs, such as the deployed
  change or credentials.>
